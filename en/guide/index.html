Record a lecture

Create a course, start recording, mark what matters and write notes while the lecturer speaks.

Step by step
  1. To record a lecture, you first need to create a course. Tap Add.
  2. Type the course name, pick a color, and add a course code if you like. Tap Create.
  3. Open the course. Tap Record.
  4. Give the lecture a name. Choose the lecture language and course.
  5. Tap Start recording, then tap the red button.
  6. The text appears while the lecturer speaks.
  7. Is the lecturer saying something important? Tap Bookmark. You can also write a note.
  8. Tap Stop when the lecture is over, and then Save.
  9. Then Ripsa summarizes the lecture for you.

Record an online lecture

Following the lecture on your computer, for example in Zoom or Teams? Then Ripsa records the sound straight from your computer.

Step by step
  1. Click Record.
  2. Choose System audio. Ripsa then records the sound from your computer.
  3. Click Start recording, and then the red button.
  4. The text appears while the lecturer speaks.
  5. Click Stop when the lecture is over, and then Save.

Material during the lecture

Add the lecturer's slides while you record, and they are included in the summary.

Step by step
  1. Do you have the slides from the lecture? Tap the paperclip.
  2. Choose file, and then the slides.
  3. Tap Stop when the lecture is over. Ripsa places the pages in the transcript when the lecture is ready.
  4. Tap Save. The slides are included in the summary.
Step by step
  1. Do you have the slides from the lecture? Click Add material.
  2. The slides show up next to your notes.
  3. Go to the page the lecturer is showing. Click Pin, and you will find the page in the transcript later.
  4. Click Stop when the lecture is over.
  5. Click Save. The slides are included in the summary.

Material after the lecture

Did you get the slides after the lecture? Ripsa puts the pages in the right place in the transcript.

Step by step
  1. Open the lecture.
  2. Tap Transcript.
  3. Tap Add material and choose the file.
  4. Tap Place everything automatically. Ripsa puts each page where the lecturer talks about it.

Study with flashcards

Ripsa makes flashcards from your lectures. You can also add your own cards.

Step by step
  1. Ripsa automatically makes a few flashcards from every lecture. Open the course.
  2. Tap Study and then Flashcards.
  3. Tap Study to start.
  4. Think about the answer. Then flip the card.
  5. Answer Knew it or Didn't know. Ripsa keeps track of the cards you know.
  6. You can also add more cards yourself. Tap plus.
  7. Write the question and the answer. Tap Add card.

Use your phone as a microphone

Sitting at the back? Put your phone close to the lecturer and follow the text on your computer. You need Ripsa on both.

Step by step
  1. Recording on your phone? Then you can follow the text on your computer.
  2. Open Ripsa on your computer. The phone's recording shows at the top. Click Follow.
  3. Now the computer is transcribing. Your phone says so too.
  4. The text appears on your computer while the lecturer speaks.
  5. Put the phone close to the lecturer and read the text on your computer instead.
  6. Stop the recording on your phone when the lecture is over, and tap Save.
