Record your lecture

Create a course, start recording, mark what matters and write notes while the lecturer speaks.

Step by step
  1. Keep your lectures together in a course. Tap Add.
  2. Type the course name and pick a color. Tap Create.
  3. Open the course. Tap Record.
  4. Give the lecture a name. Then tap the red button.
  5. The text appears while the lecturer speaks.
  6. Is the lecturer saying something important? Tap Bookmark. You can also write a note.
  7. Tap Stop when the lecture is over, and then Save.
  8. Then Ripsa summarizes the lecture for you.
