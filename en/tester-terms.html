Terms

1. The parties and the service

These terms are between you and Enjoy Solutions AB (reg. no. 559213-5007), which provides Ripsa: apps that record and transcribe lectures and create summaries, flashcards and answers to questions about the content. You accept the terms when you sign up for the trial.

2. A trial

The trial is free. Ripsa is being developed, so features may change, appear or go away, and the service may at times be unavailable or contain errors. Transcripts and summaries are made automatically and can be wrong. Check what matters to you against the source.

3. Your account

We add you and you sign in with a one-time link sent to your email address. The account is personal. The trial lasts 30 days from when we add you. We may extend or end it, and you can end it whenever you like by contacting us.

4. How you may use Ripsa

  • Use Ripsa for your own studies.
  • Follow your university's and your lecturer's rules on recording. Ask if you are unsure.
  • Only record what you are allowed to record, and do not share recordings or transcripts without permission.
  • Do not try to get around the service's protections or load it in a way that disturbs others.

If you break these terms, we may close the account.

5. Your content

What you record and create is yours. We process it only to provide the service, as described in the section on your data below.

6. Moving to your university

If your university later starts using Ripsa, we can, if you ask, move what you created during the trial to your account at the university. From the move on, the university is responsible for that data.

7. Liability

The service is provided as is, without warranties. We are not liable for lost data, wrong transcripts or other harm from using the service during the trial, except in cases of intent or gross negligence. Keep your own copies of what matters.

How we handle your data

Who is responsible?

If you try Ripsa on your own, Enjoy Solutions AB (reg. no. 559213-5007) is the data controller. You can reach us at hello@ripsa.io.

What data, and why?

  • Your sign-up: your email address, when you signed up and which version of the terms you accepted. Used to add you and to show that you accepted the terms.
  • The account: your email address, for signing in with a one-time link and for messages about the trial.
  • What you create: recordings, transcripts, summaries, material you add and questions you ask, to provide the service.
  • Technical information: IP address, device type and error reports, for security and troubleshooting.

The legal basis is the agreement between us, that is, these terms. We show no ads, build no advertising profiles and never sell data.

Where?

All processing happens inside the EU, with the providers we use for all of Ripsa: Hetzner (Finland and Germany) for servers and email, Scaleway (France) for storage and summaries, Verda (Finland) for transcription and Brevo (France and Belgium) for sending email.

For how long?

  • Your sign-up is kept until we have added you or said no.
  • Audio is normally deleted once the transcript and summary are ready.
  • What you created is deleted automatically 30 days after the trial ends, unless you have asked us to move it to your university.
  • You can delete single recordings and material in the app at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to the processing, and get a copy of the data you gave us. Contact hello@ripsa.io. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
